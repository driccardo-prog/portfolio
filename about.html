I'm Loli,

(or Lo, Loló, Lolita.)

Senior graphic designer specialized in branding, art direction and creative campaigns. I studied at Universidad de Palermo and have been working in the industry since 2017.

For over 8 years I've been translating complex identities into coherent visual systems. My career was built on 360 campaigns, key visuals, spots and countless brands. Always learning that graphic design is narrative and coherence, not just something pretty.

I started as a freelancer, worked at agencies that shaped my practice intensely, and now I balance my role at Pernod Ricard with independent projects.

I want to collaborate with people and brands that demand the best from me. Projects that challenge, where I can propose ideas and be behind every detail.

Good design is rooted in a balanced blend of Strategy & Coherence. In that I believe.

Branding | Art Direction | Creative Direction | Campaign Design | Visual Identity | Typography | Brand Strategy | Digital & Offline |

Beyond design: I'm passionate about animals, music —singing and playing guitar— and the constant act of creating. Creativity doesn't stop when I leave the computer.

If you think we could do cool stuff together, you know where to find me ;)
